# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

## Introduction

Online Now ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

Online Now keeps connection history and tip confirmations on your device. We do not operate an analytics or advertising service. Network checks and App Store purchases contact the providers described below.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be completely transparent: Online Now does not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Device identifiers or fingerprints
- Usage analytics or behavior tracking
- Network credentials or passwords
- Browsing history or app usage data

## How the App Works

Online Now monitors your device's network connectivity status using Apple's built-in Network framework. This includes:

- **Connection Status:** Whether your device is connected to the internet
- **Connection Type:** WiFi, Cellular, or Ethernet
- **Possible VPN:** An estimate based on tunnel interface names, not a guarantee of VPN protection
- **Network Quality:** Latency and speed measurements

Connection results are displayed and saved locally. To verify internet access, the app sends requests to Google, Cloudflare, and Apple. Those providers receive standard network information, including the source IP address. Download tests contact Cloudflare only when you choose Test Speed.

## Local Data Storage

The App stores your connection history locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data:

- Is not uploaded by the app; you can choose to export and share a CSV file
- Is not accessible to us or any third party
- Can be deleted at any time through the app or by deleting the app
- May be included in device backups according to your Apple backup settings

## Third-Party Services

Online Now does NOT use any third-party services for:

- Analytics or crash reporting
- Advertising or marketing
- Social media integration
- Any form of data collection or tracking

## Network Requests

The App may make the following network requests for functionality purposes only:

- **Reachability Check:** A small request to verify internet connectivity (e.g., to detect captive portals)
- **Speed Test:** Download test data to measure connection speed (only when you initiate a speed test)

These requests expose standard connection information, such as your IP address, to the responding provider. The app does not attach a user account, advertising identifier, browsing history, or saved connectivity history.

## Optional Tips

Apple handles optional consumable purchases through the App Store under its own privacy terms. The app stores verified transaction identifiers and a support count locally to avoid duplicate confirmations and handle refunds. It does not receive your payment card details. Finished consumable tips cannot be restored after reinstalling.

## Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The App is safe for use by people of all ages.
